After the March cumulative update, users reported Wi-Fi connections dropping randomly and failing to reconnect automatically. The issue particularly affected Intel Wi-Fi 6 and 6E adapters. Microsoft's suggested workaround was to "forget the network and reconnect" — the IT equivalent of "have you tried turning it off and on again?"
