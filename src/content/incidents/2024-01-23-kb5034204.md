The January 2024 cumulative update triggered unexpected Bitlocker recovery screens on boot for many users. PCs with Bitlocker encryption enabled would suddenly demand the recovery key, leaving users locked out of their machines if they didn't have it handy. Microsoft acknowledged the issue and recommended users retrieve their recovery key from their Microsoft account — a real comfort when you can't log in.
