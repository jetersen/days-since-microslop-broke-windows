October's update achieved the remarkable feat of failing to install AND failing to roll back. Users were stuck in an endless update-fail-rollback-fail cycle that consumed hours of time and several reboots. The system would attempt the update, fail at some percentage, try to undo changes, fail at that too, and then try the update again. Microsoft's eventual advice: use the Media Creation Tool to do a repair install. For a monthly patch.
