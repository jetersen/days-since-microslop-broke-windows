February's Patch Tuesday gift: USB audio devices stopped working after the update. DACs, headsets, and audio interfaces would fail to initialize or produce distorted output. The audio stack changes introduced a regression that affected a wide range of USB audio class-compliant devices. Musicians and podcasters had a particularly fun time discovering this mid-session.
