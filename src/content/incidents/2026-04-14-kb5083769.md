April 2026's Patch Tuesday hit on multiple fronts simultaneously. Some machines restarted into pixelated screens and fell into BSOD death loops. Others prompted for BitLocker recovery keys due to a misconfigured TPM validation policy — first boot after the update, no warning, just a recovery key prompt most users hadn't saved. Some systems never showed a login screen at all: the PIN and password input box simply didn't appear after reboot. Meanwhile, Background Intelligent Transfer Service (BITS) and SOAP downloads started timing out across affected clients, with bitsadmin freezing entirely — breaking any application that relied on BITS for auto-updates, including AutoCAD Advance Steel. A reboot temporarily restored downloads, but failures returned after a period of idle. Workarounds for the BITS issue are limited to disabling the service or uninstalling the update entirely.
