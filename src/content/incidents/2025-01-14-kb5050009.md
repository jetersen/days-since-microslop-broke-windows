The January 2025 update broke authentication for Citrix session recording agent users. Anyone using Citrix in enterprise environments found themselves unable to sign into Windows. Microsoft's "Known Issues" page casually listed this as affecting "some users" when it was, in practice, every single Citrix deployment that had updated. The workaround was to uninstall the update, which kind of defeats the purpose of security patches.
