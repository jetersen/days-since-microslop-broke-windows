Microsoft's May update broke the built-in OpenSSH client, causing all SSH connections to fail with cryptic error messages. Developers and sysadmins who had finally started trusting Windows' built-in SSH were rudely reminded why PuTTY refuses to die. The regression was in the key exchange algorithm negotiation, meaning even previously-working connections to known hosts would fail.
