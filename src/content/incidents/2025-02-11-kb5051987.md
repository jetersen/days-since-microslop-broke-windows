February's update introduced random USB device disconnection events. Mice, keyboards, and storage devices would momentarily disconnect and reconnect, causing input interruptions and potential data corruption on USB drives. The issue was traced to a power management regression in the USB hub driver. Just another day in the Windows ecosystem.
