In a proud tradition stretching back decades, Microsoft once again broke printing. The November update caused the Print Spooler service to crash when attempting to print to network printers. Users in enterprise environments with shared printers were particularly affected. At this point, breaking printing after a Windows update is less of a bug and more of a feature.
