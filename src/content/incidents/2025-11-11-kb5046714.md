The November 2025 update broke File Explorer's built-in zip file handling. Attempting to open or extract a zip file would crash Explorer entirely. The irony of Microsoft breaking their own archive support — which they spent years building into the OS as a selling point over third-party tools — was not lost on anyone. 7-Zip downloads spiked.
