July's update brought back an old classic: the Blue Screen of Death. This time it specifically targeted users with dual-monitor setups, particularly those with mixed refresh rates or mixed resolution displays. The BSOD would occur during display mode switches or when waking from sleep. Microsoft's workaround: "use a single monitor." Progress.
